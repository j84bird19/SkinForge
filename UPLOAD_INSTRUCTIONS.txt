SKINFORGE BODY BUILDER UPDATE

Upload these three files to the ROOT of your SkinForge GitHub repository:
- index.html
- styles.css
- body-builder.js

Replace index.html and styles.css when GitHub asks.
body-builder.js is new.

Do not delete or replace app.js, manifest.json, service-worker.js, or the icons folder.

What this build adds:
- New Body Builder main workspace
- Character bases: human, animal, plant, fruit/food, robot, alien, monster, custom
- Presentation and body presets
- Adjustable body proportions
- Detailed head and face controls
- Eye, lip, and body colors
- Horns, ears, tail, leaves, antennae, and extra eyes
- Live SVG character preview
- Randomize and reset
- “Add Body to Canvas” converts the constructed body into a new image layer
- Existing SkinForge tools remain intact
- Previous desktop-fit corrections remain included
